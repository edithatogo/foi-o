# FOI-O licence map

FOI-O uses a split licence for material owned by the repository contributors.

| Material | Licence |
| --- | --- |
| Code, scripts, tests, repository-owned synthetic code/schema fixtures, JSON schemas, JSON-LD contexts, SHACL schemas, and other executable or machine-validation schemas | [MIT](LICENSE-CODE.md) |
| Documentation, ontology terms, vocabularies, mappings, diagrams, and repository-owned descriptive metadata | [CC BY 4.0 (Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International)](LICENSE-CONTENT.md) |

Directory and file-specific notices take precedence over this summary. A mixed
file without an explicit classification is not eligible for redistribution
until its repository-owned and third-party components have been reviewed.
`pyproject.toml` and the `license` field in `CITATION.cff` describe the
MIT-licensed software distribution; they do not relicense the CC BY 4.0
semantic or documentation assets. A release manifest may separately classify
the repository-owned citation metadata file itself as CC BY 4.0.

## Third-party and source-content boundary

These licences apply only to material owned by the repository contributors.
Third-party material, source content, archived pages, legislation, official
guidance, correspondence, attachments, and derived evidence remain subject to
their original rights, notices, and terms. Inclusion of a hash, citation,
mapping, provenance record, or validation result does not grant permission to
copy or redistribute the underlying source.

Files with their own notice or an entry in a source-rights register retain that
specific status. Nothing in this repository claims new rights over third-party
or source content.
