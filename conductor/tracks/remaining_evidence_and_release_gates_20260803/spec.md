# Specification

The track provides one dependency-ordered view of remaining NZ empirical,
Australian source/rights, AU-CTH remediation, and release/publication gates.
It must preserve historical evidence, distinguish metadata from source
content, and prevent downstream execution or external mutation when an
upstream gate is unresolved.

Acceptance requires repository-owned plans, exact artifact references, explicit
human/external gate statuses, and passing Conductor validation. It does not
require or imply source capture, credentials, publication, redistribution,
profile promotion, or legal certification.

The resolution plan must define, for every remaining gate, at least one
recommended route and one fail-closed contingency; compare material trade-offs;
identify evidence inputs and outputs; name stop conditions and accountable
decision owners; and preserve dependency order across rights, empirical, and
release actions. Markdown/Mermaid and BPMN 2.0 representations must remain
consistent with that dependency model.
