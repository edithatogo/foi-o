# FOI-O NZ semantic-core scope

Version: 0.1.0 candidate

This artifact provides machine-readable workflow classifications and validation
constraints for the FOI-O NZ semantic profile. Terms such as `certified`,
`human certification`, and `decision-like event` describe FOI-O evidence and
process states only. They are not legal advice, statutory determinations,
certification of legal compliance, or proof that a person holds legal authority.

The archive contains no authentic request/source content, empirical records,
attachments, credentials, training data, or legal conclusions. Its per-file
licences are authoritative only for repository-owned material and do not alter
third-party or source-content rights.
